---
title: The assistant needs no key, and Claude connects without Neo open
date: 2026-09-22
---

Two things that used to need setting up now simply work. The assistant runs in Neo
Cloud, so there is no API key to paste in. And Claude connects to Neo Cloud directly,
so it can read and change your work whether or not Neo is open.

## The assistant needs no API key

Open the panel with `⌘J` and ask. There is nothing to set up first: Neo Cloud runs the
assistant on its own key, and your message and its answer are all that travel.

- **5 messages a day on a free account.** The footer of the panel says how many are
  left. When they are gone, the panel says so — Neo Pro, with more, is coming soon —
  and the count starts again at midnight.
- **A turn finishes even if you close the lid.** It runs in Neo Cloud, so the whole
  answer is in the conversation when you come back.
- **It still asks before every change**, in a sentence, and nothing is written until
  you say yes. What it writes shows up on your other Macs as well.

## Claude connects without Neo open

**Settings → Claude** now gives you one address, `https://sync.neomoon.io/mcp`, with
a copy button and the steps for Claude Desktop, claude.ai and Claude Code. Claude signs
in to your account in the browser and talks to Neo Cloud itself, so it works from any
machine, with Neo open or not. Each Claude you connect shows up under **Settings →
Account**, where you can sign it out.

If you set up Claude Desktop with an earlier Neo, the old connector is still in its
configuration and no longer works. The Claude pane offers to take it out — only that
one entry — and then you add the address instead.

## Recording

- **Nothing to choose any more.** Neo Cloud transcribes and writes the recap itself.
  Workspace settings keep the language your meetings are in and what the recap should
  look for.
- **60 minutes of transcription a day on a free account.** A recording past that is
  not lost and not failed: the meeting says it is waiting, and transcription carries
  on by itself the next day.

## What is gone

- **The workspace API key and the model choice**, under Assistant and Recording in
  workspace settings. A key you saved there has been deleted from Neo Cloud.
- **The Claude Desktop connector built into Neo**, and the `.mcpb` extension. The
  remote connector replaces both.
