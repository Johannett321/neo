---
title: Draw your team, share a workspace, and keep working offline
date: 2026-10-02
---

- [orgchart] **Draw the team** — Each project has a Team tab: drag people onto a board, into boxes or under each other.
- [people] **Share a workspace** — Invite people from Workspace settings → Members and see their changes as they happen.
- [cloudOff] **Keep working offline** — Neo opens on what it last saw and sends your changes when Neo Cloud is back.
- [everywhere] **All workspaces** — Everything late or due, from every workspace, on one screen with `⇧⌘1`.
- [chat] **Comments on a card** — Talk a task through at the bottom of the task itself.
