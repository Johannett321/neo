---
title: Draw your team, share a workspace, and keep working offline
date: 2026-10-02
---

A project's people now live on a board you build yourself, a workspace can be shared
with the people you work with, and Neo carries on when the connection does not.

## Draw the team

Each project has a **Team** tab — the project's people, on a board. Drag someone from
the list onto it; drop them on somebody's card and they report to them, or into a box
("Design", "Backend") and they join it. Boxes go inside boxes, and trees and boxes mix
freely. Start from **As a tree** or **In boxes** and adjust, rather than starting blank.

- **Select several** with Shift- or ⌘-click, or hold the key and drag a frame. Move
  them together, `⌘G` to put them in a box, `⌫` to take them off.
- **Copy and paste** with `⌘C` and `⌘V` — between projects too — and `⌘D` to duplicate.
- **Add person** in the toolbar adds someone to the project. The old People tab is gone;
  its links land here.

## Share a workspace

**Workspace settings → Members** makes an invite link. Whoever opens it with
**Join a workspace** in the workspace switcher sees and edits everything in that
workspace, and changes show up for everybody as they happen. The owner invites, removes
and renames; anyone can leave. The log says who did what.

## Keep working offline

What you last saw stays on this Mac, so Neo opens and works without a connection.
Changes made offline wait in line and are sent, in order, when Neo Cloud is back — the
header says how many are waiting. Saving a task, a person or a decision also feels
instant now: it shows straight away rather than after the round trip.

## Also new

- **All workspaces** (`⇧⌘1`): everything late or due, from every workspace, on one screen.
- **Today**: tick a task done (with Undo), move it to another column or cancel it from
  the right-click menu. *Show on the board* lands on the card and lights it.
- **Comments** on a card, at the bottom of the task.
- **Open questions** on a project's Decisions page — decide one and it becomes a decision.
- **Settings** open over the whole window, and close back to where you were.
- **Needs a look** only mentions a project that has been quiet for over a month.
