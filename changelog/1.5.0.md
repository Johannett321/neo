---
title: Pictures and links in notes
date: 2026-09-07
---

- [image] **Pictures in notes** — Drop or paste one in, and drag its corner to size it.
- [link] **Links between notes** — Type `[[` to link a note; what links back is listed at the bottom.
- [quote] **Callouts and tables** — Drawn as you type, in Obsidian's vocabulary.
- [edit] **A better editor** — Nested lists that renumber themselves, and `⌥↑` `⌥↓` to move a line.
- [mic] **Headsets record alone** — A note dictated through AirPods with nothing playing is no longer empty.
