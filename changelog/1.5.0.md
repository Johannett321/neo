---
title: Pictures and links in notes
date: 2026-09-07
---

Notes can now hold pictures and point at each other, the editor has grown up, and a
recording made through AirPods with nothing playing no longer comes back empty.

## What changed

- **Drop or paste a picture into a note** — a screenshot straight from `⌘⇧4` works.
  Drag its corner to size it; the width is written into the Markdown the way
  Obsidian writes one, and the picture is copied into your Markdown mirror beside
  the note so it shows there too.
- **`[[Links]]` between notes** — type `[[` to pick a note in the project, `⌘`-click
  to open it or to start one with that name. A note lists what links to it at the
  bottom, read off the text rather than kept anywhere.
- **Callouts and tables** — `> [!warning] Careful` draws a tinted box in Obsidian's
  vocabulary of kinds, and a table is drawn as one while you type in it.
- **A better editor** — nested lists that step in with their bullet, numbered lists
  that renumber themselves, `⌥↑` / `⌥↓` to move a line, shortcuts for code, strike,
  checkboxes, list kinds and heading levels, and a page copied from a browser pastes
  as Markdown rather than as its words alone.
- **Recording through a Bluetooth headset works with nothing playing.** A headset
  only offers its microphone in its lower-rate mode, and the capture of the
  computer's own sound was holding it in the other one — so a meeting recorded but a
  note dictated alone was empty. The microphone is now opened first, and the
  computer's side is no longer at risk of being played back at double speed when the
  headset switches over.
