---
title: Sync stores rows, not ciphertext
date: 2026-09-09
---

Syncing has been rebuilt. Neo used to seal every change under a key the sync server
never had, and send it as an append-only log of encrypted operations. That is gone:
the server now holds your work as ordinary rows in ordinary tables, and it can read
them.

This is a real trade and it is worth stating plainly rather than burying. The old
design made a strong promise and cost everything else — nothing on the server could
be queried, joined, indexed, repaired or even looked at, and every question about
your data had to be answered on a machine that could open it. Sync was hard to build
on and harder to fix. What you get back is a system that can grow: sharing, a web
view, recovering a workspace somebody deleted by mistake.

**If you would rather nobody else could read your work, run your own sync server.**
It is the same code as the hosted one, nothing is held back for it, and the sync
server's README says exactly what it can see. Encrypting particular columns rather
than all of them is the direction from here.

## What changed

- **There is no passphrase any more.** A passkey is the whole of signing in — Touch
  ID, or your phone, and nothing to type. The passphrase existed only to open
  ciphertext, and there is none.
- **Neo says when it is offline.** A line above the workspace switcher, and only when
  a sync server cannot be reached: *Offline — 3 changes waiting*. Everything keeps
  working at full speed, which it always did; now the app says so instead of leaving
  you to wonder. It never appears on a machine that has no account.
- **Your workspace's assistant key now syncs**, so the assistant works on a second
  Mac without being set up again. It is stored in plaintext on the sync server, along
  with everything else.
- **A conflict is resolved per row rather than per field.** If two machines edit the
  same project while both are offline, the later edit wins whole — previously one
  could change the name and the other the deadline and both survived. Editing
  different things, or the same thing at different times, behaves as it did.
- **Nothing about working offline has changed.** Neo has never needed an account and
  still does not. Every read comes out of the database on your own Mac.

## What you need to do

The sync server's accounts were reset along with its storage, so **this Mac is signed
out and your old passkey no longer works**. Open Settings → Sync, press Connect, and
choose *Create an account* — it will make a fresh passkey. Everything on this Mac
then goes up on its own.

Do the same on any other machine, signing in with the passkey you just made. Nothing
on any of them is lost in the meantime: your work lives in `~/.neo` and is only ever
copied to a server, never moved there.
