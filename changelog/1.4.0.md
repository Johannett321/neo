- [canvas] **Canvas** — An infinite board of cards, groups and arrows, beside your notes.
- [link] **Connect cards** — Drag from one card to another, and label the line if it needs a word.
- [folder] **An open format** — Stored as JSON Canvas, so it opens unchanged in Obsidian.
