---
title: Move projects between workspaces, and save without waiting
date: 2026-10-10
---

- [arrowRight] **Move a project** — Take a project and everything in it to another of your workspaces from its settings or its card.
- [sparkle] **Saves at once** — Comments, open questions, projects, columns and meetings appear the moment you save them.
- [people] **Faces in the assignee list** — Everyone you can assign a task to shows with their picture.
- [settings] **Settings as a sheet** — App settings slide up over the page you were on.
