title: Neo updates itself now
date: 2026-09-06

- [download] **Neo updates itself** — New versions arrive in the background and install when you quit.
- [bell] **A deadline says so** — One notification a day, at an hour you choose, the morning it matters.
- [today] **A front page for Today** — A banner, a line about you, your links and the weather.
- [droplet] **Liquid Glass** — The desktop showing through the window, with a slider for how much.
- [folder] **Folders for projects** — File, group and drag projects into the order you think in.
