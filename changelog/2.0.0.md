- [cloud] **Your work lives in Neo Cloud** — Sign in once and everything is there on every Mac you sign in on.
- [refresh] **Changes show up everywhere** — No sync button and nothing to wait for: there is only one copy.
- [waveform] **Recaps that finish without you** — Closing the lid after a meeting no longer stops the recap.
- [settings] **Account and Data in Settings** — Your plan, storage and signed-in Macs, and an export of everything.
